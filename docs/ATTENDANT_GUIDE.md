# Attendant Guide - Shamba Books

As an Attendant, you are the front-line staff who interact with customers daily. This guide covers your responsibilities and workflows.

## Your Role and Permissions

**You CAN:**
- Record sales
- Receive customer payments
- View today's dashboard
- View customer balances
- View stock levels

**You CANNOT:**
- See cost prices or profit
- View analytics reports
- Void transactions
- Adjust stock
- Edit past days
- View the audit log
- Manage products or settings

## Daily Tasks

### 1. Recording a Sale

#### Walk-in Customer (Cash or M-Pesa)
1. Go to Sale screen
2. Leave customer as "Walk-in Customer"
3. Add products using +/- buttons
4. Review the total
5. Select payment method (Cash or M-Pesa)
6. Click "Complete Sale"
7. The sale is recorded and stock is deducted

#### Regular Customer
1. Go to Sale screen
2. Select the customer from the dropdown
3. Add products using +/- buttons
4. Review the total and customer's current balance
5. Choose payment method:
   - **Cash**: Customer pays now
   - **M-Pesa**: Customer pays via mobile money
   - **Pay Later (Credit)**: Customer pays later (increases debt)
   - **Use Wallet**: Use customer's prepaid balance
6. Click "Complete Sale"

#### Split Payment
If a customer wants to pay part now and part later:
1. Select the customer
2. Add products
3. Use the wallet amount first (if they have prepaid credit)
4. Select a payment method for the remainder
5. Click "Complete Sale"

### 2. Receiving a Payment

When a customer comes to pay their debt:
1. Go to Customers screen
2. Find the customer (you can filter by "Owing")
3. Click on the customer
4. Enter the payment amount
5. Select payment method (Cash or M-Pesa)
6. Review the resulting balance
7. Click "Record Payment"
8. The customer's debt is reduced (or becomes prepaid if they overpaid)

### 3. Checking Stock Levels

1. Go to Stock screen
2. View the stock list
3. Check each product's current quantity
4. Red badge means low stock or out of stock
5. Green badge means stock is healthy

### 4. Viewing Customer Balances

1. Go to Customers screen
2. View all customers with their balances
3. **Green balance**: Customer has prepaid credit
4. **Red balance**: Customer owes a debt
5. **Grey balance**: Account is settled
6. Use filters to view only Owing or Prepaid customers

## Understanding Customer Accounts

### Prepaid Credit (Positive Balance)
- Customer has paid in advance
- Shop owes them goods worth this amount
- When they buy, the prepaid balance is used first
- Any remaining balance after a sale stays as credit

### Debt (Negative Balance)
- Customer owes the shop money
- They bought on credit ("Pay Later")
- They need to make a payment to clear the debt
- Follow up with them for payment

### Settled (Zero Balance)
- Customer has no debt and no prepaid credit
- All transactions are paid for

## Quick Sale Tips

### Speed Up Sales
- Use the +/- buttons quickly
- Keep product tiles in memory for fast selection
- Know your most common products
- Use keyboard on desktop for faster entry

### Handle Common Situations

**Customer wants to pay later:**
- Select "Pay Later (Credit)" as payment method
- This increases their debt
- Warn them if they're approaching their credit limit

**Customer has prepaid credit:**
- Select "Use Wallet" to apply their balance
- If the total is more than their balance, pay the remainder
- The remaining balance decreases

**Customer is a new walk-in:**
- Leave customer as "Walk-in Customer"
- They must pay Cash or M-Pesa (no credit allowed)
- Consider adding them as a regular customer if they return often

**Customer wants to split payment:**
- Use wallet first if they have prepaid credit
- Then select Cash or M-Pesa for the remainder
- The system handles the split automatically

## Stock Awareness

### Low Stock Alerts
- Red badge on stock list means low or out of stock
- Inform your Manager when stock is low
- Don't promise products that are out of stock
- Suggest alternatives if something is unavailable

### Stock Levels on Sale Screen
- Each product tile shows current stock
- Prevents selling items you don't have
- Helps you make recommendations to customers

## Daily Dashboard

Your dashboard shows:
- **Today's Sales**: Total sales for the day
- **Cash In**: Total cash received
- **M-Pesa In**: Total M-Pesa received
- **Credit Given**: Total credit sales today
- **Quick Actions**: Buttons for common tasks

Check this throughout the day to track progress.

## Customer Service

### Sharing Statements
1. Click on a customer
2. Click "Share Statement via WhatsApp"
3. This opens WhatsApp with a summary
4. Send it to the customer
5. Helpful for reminding customers of their balance

### Adding New Customers
If a new customer wants credit:
- Ask your Manager to add them first
- You cannot add customers yourself
- Once added, you can sell to them on credit

## Best Practices

### Accuracy

- Count cash carefully
- Enter M-Pesa transaction codes correctly

### Speed
- Learn the product tiles for quick selection
- Use keyboard shortcuts on desktop
- Keep the sale flow smooth

### Customer Relations
- Be polite and helpful
- Explain balances clearly

### Integrity
- Record all sales accurately
- Don't bypass stock checks

## Troubleshooting

### Sale won't complete
- Check if the product is in stock
- Verify you selected a customer for credit sales
- Ensure a payment method is selected
- Ask your Manager for help if needed

### Customer balance seems wrong
- The balance shows their running account
- Positive = they have credit with you
- Negative = they owe you
- If unsure, ask your Manager to check

### Stock shows as out of stock
- Don't sell the item
- Inform the customer it's unavailable
- Suggest alternatives
- Tell your Manager about the shortage

### Customer disputes their balance
- Share their statement via WhatsApp
- Show them their transaction history
- If still disputed, ask your Manager to investigate
- Don't change balances yourself

## What to Do When...

### Customer wants to return an item
- You cannot process returns
- Ask your Manager to handle it
- Returns may need special approval

### Customer wants a discount
- You cannot give discounts
- Prices are set by the Owner
- Ask your Manager if there's a promotion

### You make a mistake on a sale
- You cannot void transactions
- Ask your Manager to void it for you
- Explain what happened
- Be honest about mistakes

### Cash doesn't match at end of day
- Count cash again carefully
- Check for missing payments
- Review today's sales
- Report any discrepancy to your Manager

### System is slow or not working
- Wait a moment and try again
- Check if you're offline (banner at top)
- If offline, changes will sync when back online
- Ask your Manager if issues persist
