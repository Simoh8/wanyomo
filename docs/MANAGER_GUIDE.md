# Manager Guide - Shamba Books

As a Manager, you have access to most operational features of Shamba Books. This guide covers your responsibilities and workflows.

## Your Role and Permissions

**You CAN:**
- Record sales, payments, and expenses
- Adjust stock levels
- Close the day
- View all reports and analytics
- Manage stock (receive, wastage, conversion, stocktake)
- View customer accounts and statements

**You CANNOT:**
- Manage users or invite team members
- Change product prices or costs
- View the audit log
- Access owner-level settings

## Daily Tasks

### 1. Record Sales
1. Go to Sale screen
2. Select customer (or leave as Walk-in)
3. Add products using +/- buttons
4. Choose payment method (Cash, M-Pesa, Credit, or Wallet)
5. Click "Complete Sale"
6. Stock is automatically deducted

### 2. Receive Payments
1. Go to Customers screen
2. Find the customer (use filters: All, Owing, Prepaid)
3. Click on the customer
4. Enter payment amount
5. Select payment method (Cash or M-Pesa)
6. Click "Record Payment"
7. Customer balance is updated automatically

### 3. Receive Stock
1. Go to Stock > Receive Stock
2. Select the product
3. Enter quantity received
4. Enter unit cost (optional)
5. Select source (Purchase or Farm Production)
6. Click "Record Receipt"
7. Stock level is updated

### 4. Record Wastage
1. Go to Stock > Wastage
2. Select the product
3. Enter quantity wasted
4. Select reason (Wastage, Spoilage, or Damage)
5. Click "Record Wastage"
6. Stock is deducted

### 5. Add Expenses
1. Go to Expenses screen
2. Enter amount
3. Select category
4. Choose payment method (Cash or M-Pesa)
5. Add a note (optional)
6. Click "Add Expense"

### 6. Close the Day
1. Go to Day Close
2. Select today's date
3. Review opening balances (auto-filled)
4. Review today's activity
5. Enter counted cash and M-Pesa
6. Add notes about the day
7. Click "Close Day"
8. Review variance

## Weekly Tasks

### 1. Review Stock Levels
- Go to Stock > Stock List
- Check for low stock (red badge)
- Review out-of-stock items
- Plan orders based on reorder levels

### 2. Stocktake
1. Go to Stock > Stocktake
2. Count actual stock for each product
3. Enter counted quantities
4. Review variances
5. Click "Record Stocktake"
6. Stock is adjusted to match counts

### 3. Internal Conversions
1. Go to Stock > Convert
2. Select source product (e.g., Milk)
3. Select target product (e.g., Mala)
4. Enter quantity to convert
5. Set yield ratio (e.g., 0.9 for 90% yield)
6. Click "Record Conversion"
7. Stock is adjusted for both products

### 4. Review Customer Debts
1. Go to Customers
2. Filter by "Owing"
3. Review customer balances
4. Follow up with overdue customers
5. Share statements via WhatsApp if needed

### 5. Analytics Review
- Go to Analytics screen
- Select "This week"
- Review sales trends
- Check payment method breakdown
- Analyze expenses by category

## Managing Customer Accounts

### Understanding Customer Balances
- **Positive balance**: Customer has prepaid credit (shop owes them goods)
- **Negative balance**: Customer owes a debt
- **Zero balance**: Account is settled

### Recording a Payment
1. Find the customer
2. Click on them to open payment modal
3. Enter the amount
4. Payment is applied to debt first
5. Any excess becomes prepaid credit
6. Review the resulting balance before saving

### Sharing Statements
1. Click on a customer
2. Click "Share Statement via WhatsApp"
3. A pre-filled message is generated
4. Customer's phone opens WhatsApp with the message
5. Send to share their statement

## Stock Management Best Practices

### Receiving Stock
- Always record stock receipts immediately
- Enter unit cost for accurate valuation
- Distinguish between purchases and farm production
- Use "Today's milking" shortcut for farm milk

### Recording Wastage
- Record wastage as soon as it happens
- Select the most accurate reason
- This helps identify recurring issues
- Regular wastage review can reduce losses

### Stocktake Tips
- Do stocktakes at the end of a day
- Count when the shop is closed
- Review variances before confirming
- Investigate significant variances with the Owner

### Internal Conversions
- Use for production processes (milk to mala)
- Set accurate yield ratios
- This tracks production efficiency
- Helps cost conversion processes

## Day Close Best Practices

### Before Closing
- Ensure all sales are recorded
- Record all expenses
- Count cash carefully
- Verify M-Pesa totals with phone
- Resolve any discrepancies

### During Close
- Review all figures
- Add detailed notes about issues
- Note any special circumstances
- Explain any variance

### After Closing
- If variance is significant, investigate
- Keep records of variances
- Report issues to Owner
- Use closed days for performance review

## Analytics and Reporting

### Key Reports to Review
- **Sales by Product**: Best and worst sellers
- **Payment Methods**: Cash vs M-Pesa vs Credit mix
- **Expenses by Category**: Where money is spent
- **Sales Trend**: Daily/weekly/monthly patterns
- **Debt Aging**: How long customers take to pay

### Using Analytics
- Select different time periods (Today, 7 days, This month, Last month)
- Compare with previous periods
- Identify trends and patterns
- Make data-driven decisions

## Troubleshooting

### Sale won't complete
- Check if stock is available
- Verify customer is selected for credit sales
- Ensure payment method is valid
- Check for negative stock (if disabled)

### Stock count doesn't match
- Run a stocktake to adjust
- Review recent stock movements
- Check for unrecorded sales or wastage
- Report to Owner if theft suspected

### Customer balance seems wrong
- View customer statement
- Check for unrecorded payments
- Verify voids were processed correctly
- Use "Recalculate balance" if needed (ask Owner)

### Day close variance
- Re-count cash and M-Pesa
- Review transactions for the day
- Check for missing expenses
- Verify payment methods were correct
- Add notes explaining the variance

## Communication with Owner

### Report to Owner When:
- Significant stock variances occur
- High wastage levels are observed
- Customer debts are overdue
- Cash variance is unusual
- System errors or issues arise

### Ask Owner For:
- Product price changes
- Adding new products
- Adjusting customer credit limits
- Reopening a closed day
- Access to cost prices (if needed)

## Best Practices

1. **Be Consistent**: Record transactions as they happen
2. **Be Accurate**: Double-check quantities and amounts
3. **Be Timely**: Close the day promptly
4. **Be Thorough**: Add notes for unusual events
5. **Be Proactive**: Follow up on debts and low stock
